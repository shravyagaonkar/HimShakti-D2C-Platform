# PROMPTS.md

## AI Prompt Experiments

### Prompt Variation 1

**Prompt:**

```
Generate a product description for: Organic Honey
```

**Example Output:**
"Organic Honey is a natural sweetener collected from high-quality floral sources. It is rich in antioxidants and ideal for daily consumption."

**Observation:** Output was short and lacked marketing appeal.

---

### Prompt Variation 2

**Prompt:**

```
Generate a professional and attractive product description for Organic Honey highlighting its benefits and natural ingredients.
```

**Example Output:**
"Organic Honey is a pure and naturally sourced product known for its rich flavor and health benefits. Packed with antioxidants and nutrients, it serves as a healthy alternative to refined sugar and can be used in beverages, desserts, and daily wellness routines."

**Observation:** Better structure and informative content.

---

### Prompt Variation 3

**Prompt:**

```
You are a marketing assistant for an e-commerce platform. Write an engaging product description for Organic Honey in 80-100 words, emphasizing quality, health benefits, and customer appeal.
```

**Example Output:**
"Experience the goodness of nature with Organic Honey, carefully sourced to deliver purity and taste in every spoonful. Rich in antioxidants and natural nutrients, it supports a healthy lifestyle while adding sweetness to your favorite foods and drinks. Its smooth texture and authentic flavor make it a perfect choice for health-conscious consumers seeking a natural alternative to processed sweeteners."

**Observation:** Produced the most detailed and user-friendly descriptions.

---

## Best Prompt

Prompt Variation 3 worked best because it clearly defined the AI's role and specified the desired style and length of the output. Providing context as a marketing assistant encouraged the model to generate more engaging and customer-focused descriptions. The response was well-structured, informative, and suitable for display on an e-commerce platform.

## System Prompt / Role Used

```
You are an AI marketing assistant that generates professional product descriptions for an e-commerce website.
```
